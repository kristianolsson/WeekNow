Privacy Policy

WeekNow — Last updated: March 30, 2026

WeekNow is a simple utility app that displays the current ISO week number in your Android status bar. This policy explains what data the app does and does not collect.

Data Collection

WeekNow does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Specifically:

  • No personal information is collected
  • No device identifiers are collected
  • No location data is accessed
  • No usage analytics or crash reporting
  • No advertising

Internet Access

WeekNow does not require or use internet access. All functionality runs entirely on-device.

Permissions

The app requests the following Android permissions:

  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE — required to display a persistent notification in the status bar
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED — required to restart the service automatically after a device reboot

Neither permission is used to access or transmit any personal data.

Third-Party Services

WeekNow does not use any third-party SDKs, analytics tools, or advertising networks.

Children's Privacy

WeekNow does not collect any data from any users, including children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy is updated, the revised version will be posted at this URL with an updated date.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, you can reach out via the app's Google Play listing.
